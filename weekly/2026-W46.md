Signal vs Counter-signal

- Signal: Platform vendors are shipping first‑party agent runtimes and features (OpenAI Wallet, Gemini at Work, AWS Bedrock AgentCore, Vercel agent-browser v0.43.0) — this favors platform-default MCP/orchestration and exporter defaults. Evidence strength: Strong (vendor posts/releases).
- Counter-signal: Market still shows fragmented connectors, pricing, and no single memory primitive; many infra primitives are single-repo and lack multi-vendor adoption.
- Current interpretation: Evidence favors platform-first defaults (↑ confidence for thesis 8), but widespread fragmentation and single-repo infra keep operational heterogeneity. What would flip it: broad cross-vendor MCP compatibility standard or a major vendor turning MCP into a managed commodity with enforced exporter/retention defaults.
